Privacy & cookies

We collect as little as we can.

Last updated 20 November 2026

What we collect

You can read every explainer and use every calculator without an account. Numbers you type into a calculator stay in your browser, they are not sent to us or stored.

If you email us, we keep your email address and message only to reply and to handle corrections or topic requests.

Cookies

  • Essential: remembers your cookie choice (ts_consent, 12 months). The site can’t work properly without it.
  • Analytics (optional): only used if you choose “Accept all”. It tells us, in aggregate, which pages are useful. You can change your mind at any time.

Services we rely on

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them. We don’t sell, rent or share your information for advertising.

Security

Every page is served over HTTPS with modern security headers. We don’t ask for your TFN, bank details or passwords, and never will.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted, email hello@taxsignal.com.au. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Privacy & cookies

Your privacy, kept simple.

Last updated 20 November 2026

No accounts, no personal data

You don’t need to sign up or log in to use TaxSignal. We don’t ask for your name, TFN, bank details or any other personal information to read our guides or use our calculators.

Your numbers stay with you

Everything you type into a calculator is worked out on your own device. It isn’t sent to us, saved or shared.

Cookies

We use one small cookie to remember your cookie choice, so we don’t ask you again on every visit. If you choose “Accept all”, we may also measure, in total, which pages people find useful. This never identifies you, and you can change your choice at any time.

Secure by default

The whole site runs over an encrypted, secure connection. Like any website, our hosting provider keeps basic technical records for a short time to protect the site from misuse. We never sell, rent or share information with advertisers.

Getting in touch

If you email us, we use your message only to reply to you. Questions about privacy? Email hello@taxsignal.com.au. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles.

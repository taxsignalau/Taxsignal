The guide

Tax invoice or invoice? It depends on GST.

Registered for GST

Send a tax invoice. Put the words "Tax invoice" at the top, show your ABN, and show the GST amount. Your customer needs it to claim GST credits, so they may ask for one.

Not registered

Send an invoice and don't charge GST. Still quote your ABN, or a business paying you more than $75 (excluding GST) may have to withhold 47% of the payment.

Keep a copy

Keep copies of the invoices you send for five years. Download each one as a PDF and store it with your business records.

Questions

Common questions about invoices.

What must a tax invoice include?

For a sale under $1,000: the words "Tax invoice", your business name and ABN, the date, a description of what you sold with quantity and price, the GST amount, and whether each item is taxable. For $1,000 or more, also include the buyer's name or ABN.

I'm not registered for GST. What do I send?

Send an invoice, not a tax invoice, and don't add GST to your prices. Include your ABN. If you don't quote it, a business paying you more than $75 (excluding GST) may have to withhold 47% of the payment.

When do I have to register for GST?

When your GST turnover reaches $75,000 a year, or $150,000 for non-profits. Taxi and ride-sourcing drivers must register whatever their turnover.

Do I have to give my customer a tax invoice?

If you're registered for GST and a customer asks for one, you must provide it within 28 days. You don't need to for sales of $82.50 or less, including GST.

Is my invoice saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so you can come back to a draft. Nothing is sent to TaxSignal or stored on a server. Clearing your browser data removes it.

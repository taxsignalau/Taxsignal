About TaxSignal

TaxSignal explains Australian tax in plain English, for taxpayers, business owners, students and practitioners. Every explanation is built on the legislation and the ATO’s own guidance, and every source is linked.

§ 1 Editorial standards

How we keep it accurate.

  1. 01 Primary sources firstExplanations are built on the legislation, ATO rulings and guidance, Treasury material and the Federal Register of Legislation.
  2. 02 Status is always shownEvery update sits on the pathway from proposed to commenced. A proposal is never described as law.
  3. 03 Dates you can checkEach page shows when it was published, when it takes effect and when it was last verified against its source.
  4. 04 Every source linkedOriginal documents are linked directly so you can read the authoritative text yourself.
  5. 05 Reviewed before publishingAI may help draft summaries, but nothing is published until it has been checked against the source by a person.
§ 2 Who’s behind it

Written by Australian tax professionals.

Written in Australia by tax professionals who prefer to stay in the background and let the work speak. Every explanation links to the legislation and ATO guidance it relies on, so you never have to take our word for it.

§ 3 What we don’t do

TaxSignal provides general information only. It is not tax, legal or financial advice, and it does not consider your circumstances. For advice, speak to a registered tax agent. TaxSignal is not affiliated with the Australian Taxation Office.

§ 4 Contact

Spotted an error? Want a topic covered?

Corrections are reviewed against the source and the update’s “last verified” date is changed. We can’t answer questions about individual tax situations.

Bill introducedTrusts Discretionary trusts: Senate passes the 30% minimum tax, with changes for testamentary trusts The Senate passed the bills for a 30% minimum tax on the net income of discretionary trusts on 25 November 2026, with Government amendments to how testamentary trusts are treated. The amended bills now return to the House of Representatives. This is not yet law. Effective 1 July 2028 (proposed) · 2028–29 onwards (proposed) Source: Parliament of Australia; AAP; Sladen Legal · Verified 25 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Property & Investments Royal Assent Effective 1 July 2027
2027–28 onwards Negative gearing and CGT: what changes from 1 July 2027 Negative gearing will be limited to new builds, and the 50% CGT discount replaced by cost base indexation and a 30% minimum tax on gains that accrue after 1 July 2027. The changes are law. Source: ATO; Budget 2026–27 · Verified 22 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Fringe Benefits Tax Bill introduced Effective 1 April 2027 (proposed)
FBT year 2027–28 onwards (proposed) Electric car FBT exemption to narrow from 1 April 2027 The full FBT exemption for electric cars continues until 31 March 2027. Under a bill now in Parliament, cars over $75,000 would then get a 25% discount instead, and from 1 April 2029 the 25% discount would apply to all eligible electric cars. Source: ATO; Budget 2026–27 · Verified 22 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Small Business Announced Effective 1 July 2026 (write-off) · 1 July 2027 (instalments)
2026–27 onwards Budget 2026–27: $20,000 instant asset write-off made permanent, monthly PAYG instalments The Budget makes the $20,000 instant asset write-off permanent from 1 July 2026 for businesses with turnover up to $10 million, and lets businesses opt in to monthly PAYG instalments from 1 July 2027. Source: Budget 2026–27 · Verified 22 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Superannuation Commenced Effective 1 July 2026
2026–27 onwards Payday Super has started: super is now due every payday Since 1 July 2026, employers must pay super guarantee contributions each payday, and the money must reach the employee’s fund within 7 business days. Source: ATO; Federal Register of Legislation · Verified 22 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Superannuation Passed Effective 1 July 2026
2026–27 onwards Division 296: extra tax on earnings for super balances above $3 million Parliament passed the Division 296 bills on 10 March 2026. An additional tax applies to realised earnings attributable to total super balances above $3 million, with a higher rate above $10 million. Source: Treasury; Parliament of Australia · Verified 19 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Personal Income Tax Commenced Effective 1 July 2026 (15%) · 1 July 2027 (14%)
2026–27 and 2027–28 The 16% tax rate is now 15%, falling to 14% next year From 1 July 2026, income between $18,201 and $45,000 is taxed at 15%. The rate falls to 14% from 1 July 2027. Source: ATO; Federal Register of Legislation · Verified 19 Nov 2026 Read the explainer → Superannuation Commenced Effective 1 July 2025
2025–26 onwards Super guarantee rate reached 12% The super guarantee rate rose to 12% on 1 July 2025, the final legislated step in the schedule of increases. Source: ATO · Verified 19 Nov 2026 Read the explainer →

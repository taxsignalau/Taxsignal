§ 1  The figuresIncome year 2026–27

Key rates and thresholds for 2026–27.

Each figure links back to the Act that sets it, so you can check the law yourself.

$18,200
Tax-free threshold
The first $18,200 of an Australian resident’s taxable income is taxed at nil.
Source: Income Tax Rates Act 1986
15%
On income from $18,201 to $45,000
Down from 16% on 1 July 2026, and legislated to fall to 14% from 1 July 2027.
Source: Income Tax Rates Act 1986
12%
Super guarantee
Now payable every payday under Payday Super, reaching the fund within 7 business days.
Source: Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992
10%
GST on taxable supplies
One-eleventh of a GST-inclusive price.
Source: A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999
47%
Fringe benefits tax rate
For the FBT year 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027.
Source: Fringe Benefits Tax Act 1986
25%
Base rate entity company tax
Turnover under $50 million and mostly active income. All other companies pay 30%.
Source: Income Tax Rates Act 1986, s 23

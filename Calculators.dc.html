§  How the income tax calculator worksResident rates 2025–26 and 2026–27

Tax on each slice of income, less offsets, plus Medicare.

  1. 01 Tax each slice of taxable income at its rate: nil to $18,200, then 15% (16% in 2025–26) to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above.
  2. 02 Subtract the low income tax offset, up to $700, phasing out completely at $66,667.
  3. 03 Add the 2% Medicare levy, unless you hold an exemption.
  4. 04 Add the Medicare levy surcharge if your income is above the threshold and you do not hold appropriate private hospital cover.

Questions people ask

What are the tax rates for 2026–27?+

For Australian residents: nil on the first $18,200, 15% from $18,201 to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above $190,000. The 15% rate falls to 14% from 1 July 2027.

What is the low income tax offset?+

The low income tax offset reduces tax by up to $700 for taxable incomes up to $37,500. It reduces by 5 cents per dollar between $37,500 and $45,000, then by 1.5 cents per dollar until it reaches nil at $66,667.

Who pays the Medicare levy surcharge?+

Higher-income earners without appropriate private hospital cover pay an extra 1% to 1.5%. For 2026–27 it starts above $105,000 for singles and $210,000 for families.

Is this my final tax bill?+

No. It is an estimate of tax on taxable income. Your actual tax also depends on deductions, other offsets, study loan repayments and the tax already withheld from your pay.
